Privacy

D2 Allrounder is a free, non-commercial fan app for Destiny 2. It has no server of its own and no user accounts: everything runs in your browser.

What is stored, and where

  • Bungie sign-in. You sign in on bungie.net. Bungie gives the app an access token, which is kept only in your browser (localStorage). The app never sees your password.
  • Settings. Filters, panel width, optimizer choices and similar settings are saved in your browser. “Reset app settings” in the account panel deletes them; signing out deletes the token.
  • Your game data (characters, inventory, records, stats) is loaded directly from Bungie's API into your browser and is not sent anywhere else.

Who else is involved

  • Bungie (bungie.net): sign-in, game data and item images. Bungie privacy policy.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the app. GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address. GitHub privacy statement.
  • Image hosts: a few raid symbol images are loaded from community sites (Destinypedia, Steam). Opening those parts of the raid guide sends a normal image request to them.
  • Squad check looks up other players by their public Bungie name, using only data Bungie makes public.

No tracking

No analytics, no ads, no cookies of our own, no data sold or shared.
